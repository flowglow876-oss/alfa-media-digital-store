Refund & Replacement Policy

Last Updated: 20 October 2026 | Entity: Alfa Media Digital Store

At Alfa Media Digital Store, we take immense pride in publishing practical, high-utility business playbooks. Because the Online Business Blueprint Bundle – 7 PDF Guides is an intangible digital asset delivered immediately upon server verification, orders are handled under the fair and transparent rules detailed below.

1. Nature of Digital Products

Digital files (such as downloadable PDFs) cannot be physically returned once access has been unlocked. For this reason, we provide complete, verbatim chapter breakdowns and high-resolution sample page previews on our storefront so prospective customers can make an informed purchasing decision before buying.

2. Eligible Circumstances for Immediate Refund or Replacement

We handle support requests case by case and will promptly issue a refund or alternate delivery under any of the following circumstances:

  • Accidental Duplicate Charge: If your network experienced a delay and your bank account or UPI was charged more than once for the same bundle, email us at alfamediaautomations@gmail.com with your bank reference or transaction IDs. The duplicate transaction will be refunded in full within 48 business hours to your original payment source.
  • Technical Delivery Failure: If your payment was verified by the bank but you did not receive a functional download link on the order page within 2 hours, contact our team. We will manually generate a secure direct access link. If we fail to deliver accessible files within 12 business hours of your notice, you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Corrupted or Unreadable Files: If any downloaded PDF is damaged, fails checksum, or cannot be opened using standard PDF readers (Adobe Acrobat, Apple Books, Google Drive), our support team will provide replacement files immediately. If the issue cannot be resolved, a complete refund will be issued.

3. Ineligible Circumstances

We do not issue refunds for:

  • Change of mind after the complete 250+ page bundle has been accessed and downloaded.
  • Failure to apply the frameworks or expectations of guaranteed commercial income (as explicitly stated in our Earnings Disclaimer, results require individual execution and market demand).
  • Purchases made through unauthorized third-party links or resellers.

4. How to Request Assistance or a Refund

To submit an inquiry or refund request:

  1. Email our customer desk at alfamediaautomations@gmail.com with the subject line Refund Request - [Your Order Reference].
  2. Include your transaction reference number, mobile number used at checkout, and a brief description of the issue.
  3. Our team will acknowledge your request within 12 business hours and resolve eligible claims within 48 hours.
