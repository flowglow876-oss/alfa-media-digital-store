Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 20 October 2026 | Entity: Alfa Media Digital Store

Welcome to Alfa Media Digital Store ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate the digital storefront at this website offering digital downloads, specifically the Online Business Blueprint Bundle. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with applicable laws in India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or purchase digital products from our store, we collect only the information necessary to fulfill your order:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, delivery email address, and mobile phone number (provided during checkout for transaction receipts and SMS verification).
  • Transaction & Order Data: Opaque Order ID, amount paid (₹399 INR), timestamp, payment method type (UPI), the details read from the payment screenshot you upload (amount, receiver name, UTR/reference number, date and time), and a fingerprint of that image used to prevent reuse. We never see, store, or process your credit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or bank account passwords.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, and browser user-agent collected by our CDN (Cloudflare) for DDoS prevention, rate limiting, and security logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To cryptographically verify your bank payment and unlock digital PDF downloads.
  • To email you a link to your order page, only if you request it.
  • To respond to your customer service inquiries, duplicate charge disputes, or file extraction assistance.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions or digital intellectual property abuse.

3. Data Retention and Sharing

Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with third-party advertising brokers or data harvesting networks.

We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate this store:

  • Payment Verification: Payments are made by UPI directly to the store owner. The screenshot you upload is read by an automated AI service (Google Gemini) solely to extract the payment details above, and is not used to identify you.
  • Hosting & Edge CDN: Cloudflare Pages for encrypted TLS 1.3 delivery and static file serving.
  • Transactional Email Services: To send your order-page link if you ask for it.

4. Data Storage & Security

All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted using TLS 1.3. Download links generated upon payment are temporary, signed, and time-expiring to protect against unauthorized redistribution.

5. Your Rights

Under Indian law, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about your order, request correction of an inaccurate email address, or request deletion of your contact records after order fulfillment. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Grievance Officer at alfamediaautomations@gmail.com.
